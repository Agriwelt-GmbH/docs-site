# Switching on

What the panel does after power-on, which state it starts in, and what to wait for before switching off.

When the panel gets power, a start screen with the logo appears. The start screen stays until every device connected to the panel has answered — usually a few seconds, at most about fifteen — and then the [home screen](home-screen.md) opens.

![The start screen.](figures/five-boot)

> **NOTE**
>
> **If the start screen stays about fifteen seconds,** the panel has been waiting for a device that does not answer. Nothing is broken yet — but look at the home screen and the alarms as soon as it appears. See [How alarms work](how-alarms-work.md).

## What state the panel starts in

- **Access level: Operator.** The panel starts at the Operator level after every power-on, whatever level was set before. Switching off and on is therefore also the quickest way back to Operator — the only level at which alarm windows are shown. See [Access levels](access-levels.md).
- **The ordinary home screen.** The [second home screen](second-home-screen.md) is never opened by itself; you open it by hand.
- **Application locked, if the speed is imitated.** If speed imitation is switched on in the speed sensor and the machine has dispenser electric motors or a liquid fertilizer pump, the panel starts with product application **locked by hand**. Unlock it when you are ready: [Lock product application by hand](lock-application-by-hand.md).

The firmware version and the serial number can be read at any time: two-finger tap on the home screen, then **“About”** in the main menu. The window closes with **“Close”**.

## Before switching off

- **Settings** are stored by themselves a few seconds after your last change. Wait a few seconds after changing a setting before you switch the panel off.
- **The statistics** are written every five minutes and when the machine stops. **Switch the panel off only after you have stopped moving** — otherwise the last stretch of work is lost from the [statistics](statistics-screen.md).
