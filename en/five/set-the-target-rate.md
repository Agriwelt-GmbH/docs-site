# Change the target rate

Raise or lower the rate the panel holds and guards, from the home screen, while seeding — by touch.

The rate field at the right end of each diagram shows two numbers: **the measured rate on top, the target below it**. In the “Flow” mode the target is the alarm level of the diagram — see [The section diagram](section-diagram.md).

## On a diagram

::: steps
1. On the home screen, **touch and hold the diagram** whose rate you want to change, for more than half a second. Its target figure lights up.

   ![The seed diagram with its target figure lit for editing.](figures/five-home-highlight)

2. **Swipe up** over the screen to raise the target, **down** to lower it — one step per swipe. The target stays within its permitted limits.

3. **Tap the screen outside the diagrams** to finish. The figure stops being lit and the new target is in force.
:::

To change another diagram — fertilizer after seed, say — touch and hold that diagram instead.

## For a product applied by weight

::: steps
1. Touch and hold the kg/ha indicator of the product. The number pad opens with the caption **Product** and the number of the product.

2. Type the target, from 10.0 to 500.0 kg/ha, and touch **“OK”** ([Entering numbers](interface-basics.md#entering-numbers)). A tap outside the pad leaves the target as it was.
:::

## On the second home screen

Touch and hold the rate until it lights up, then swipe up or down. A tap elsewhere ends it. See [Second home screen](second-home-screen.md).

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **Columns under the line are not guarded.** If the target is set far above what the machine really delivers, the columns sink under the control line and the panel shows **“NOT UNDER CONTROL”**. On a grain drill, let the panel find the level: [Set the alarm level automatically](set-the-alarm-level.md).
