# Lock product application by hand

Stop all product application with one touch and hold, and start it again — what the lock does and how to see that it is on.

The manual lock stops **all product application**: the dispenser motors and the pump do not run, however the machine moves. Use it to cross a field already sown, to drive on the road with the implement lowered, or when you work [without the speed sensor](work-without-speed-sensor.md).

The lock exists on machines with **dispenser electric motors or a liquid fertilizer pump**.

::: steps
1. On the home screen, **touch and hold an empty place in the right-hand column** of indicators — not on an icon — until the panel beeps once.

2. Check the right-hand column. A **yellow hand** blinks there: application is locked.

   ![The right-hand column of the home screen with the yellow hand.](figures/five-home-lock)

3. To unlock, touch and hold the same place again. One short beep; the hand disappears.
:::

A touch and hold **on an icon** of the column opens the context menu of that device instead.

On the [second home screen](second-home-screen.md), touch and hold the **stop symbol** — the same lock, the same beep.

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **The lock is announced by the screen only.** Look for the yellow hand before you start a pass. A locked machine drives a whole field and sows nothing.

## What the lock changes elsewhere

- The motor tiles on the [Motors screen](motors-screen.md) show a yellow **STOP** — serviceable, not applying.
- The product application control does not start when you move off — the lock must be off for the control to start ([Quick start](quick-start.md#moving-off)).
- The work done while locked is counted in the statistics as **“Work without alarms control”** ([Statistics](statistics-screen.md)).

## When the panel locks by itself

Switching speed imitation on locks product application. A panel that is switched on with imitation already on starts locked as well. Unlock by hand when you are ready to apply: [Work without the speed sensor](work-without-speed-sensor.md).
