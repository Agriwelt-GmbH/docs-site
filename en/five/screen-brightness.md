# Set the screen brightness

Make the screen brighter or dimmer — one item in the Settings menu, set by hand.

::: steps
1. On the home screen tap with **two fingers**. Tap **“Settings”**, and tap it again.

2. **“Brightness,%”** is the first item. Tap it, then swipe right to brighten or left to dim, in steps of 10, from 0 to 100.

   ![The Settings menu with Brightness,% selected.](figures/five-menu-settings)

3. Tap outside the menu to leave it. The new brightness stays in force and is stored by itself.
:::

There is **no automatic brightness**: the screen stays at the value you set, day and night. Dim it for night work and raise it again in the morning. **0 is the dimmest setting, not off** — the screen stays lit.

The same menu holds **“Volume”** — the loudness of the alarm sound — and **“Buttons sound”**, the click on every touch. Everything else in the Settings menu: [The Settings menu](settings-menu.md).
