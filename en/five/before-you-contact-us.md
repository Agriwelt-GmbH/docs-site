# Before you contact support

A checklist — what to try, what to write down and which photos to attach — so that your question can be answered at once, and whom to contact first.

Most faults can be found without help. Go through this list first. If you still need help, the notes and photos below let support answer at once instead of asking you again.

## Checklist

- [ ] The [check after installation](check-after-installation.md) was done, and every step passed.
- [ ] You read the alarm window and its topic: [How alarms work](how-alarms-work.md). Alarms are shown at the **“Operator”** level only ([Access levels](access-levels.md)).
- [ ] You went through the topic of that alarm — for example [Connection lost or wrong device](alarms-any-device.md), [Sensor clogged or broken](alarms-sensor-state.md), [Speed sensor alarms](alarms-speed-sensor.md), [Fan or dispenser shaft: speed alarms](alarms-rotation.md), [Air pressure](alarms-pressure.md), [Hopper empty](alarms-hopper.md), [Motor controller alarms](alarms-motor-controller.md).
- [ ] You did **not** change the profile or the device configuration to try things out ([Device configuration](device-configuration.md)).

## Write down

- **The exact text of the alarm window**, word for word, and the name of the device in it.
- **The firmware version and the serial number:** two-finger tap on the home screen, then **“About”** ([Switching on](switching-on.md)).
- **The machine and the number of rows.**
- **The status of the device:** on its status screen, **“Link:”**, **Status:** and, for a motor, **“Last fault:”** with its code ([The sensor status screen](sensor-status-screen.md), [Motor fault codes](motor-fault-codes.md)).
- **What was done before the fault** — what you changed, mounted, connected or replaced, and when the fault first appeared.

## Photograph

- **The sensor status screen** of the device that is wrong.
- **The alarm window.**
- **How the devices are connected** — the cables and the connectors of the device and of the panel, so that the connections can be followed.
- **The result of the check after installation**, if you have it.

## Whom to contact

1. **First your dealer or installation partner.** They know your machine and how it was mounted.
2. **Then Agriwelt:** support@agriwelt.com. Send the notes and the photos in the first message.
