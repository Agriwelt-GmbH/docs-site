# The panel and its touch screen

What the Hectare 5 panel is made of from the operator's side — a touch screen without buttons — and what else is built into it.

The Hectare 5 has **no buttons**. Everything is done with the fingers on the screen: taps, touch-and-hold, swipes, and taps with two or three fingers. What each gesture does on each screen is in [Touch zones and gestures](buttons-and-gestures.md).

![The Hectare 5 panel with the home screen. The screen texts in this picture are not in English.](figures/five-panel)

## The screen

- **800 × 480 colour screen** with a touch surface. It recognises up to three fingers at once — which is how the panel tells a tap from a two-finger or a three-finger tap.
- **Brightness** is set by hand, in steps of 10 %, in the Settings menu. There is no automatic brightness. See [Set the screen brightness](screen-brightness.md).
- **Sound.** The panel clicks on every touch it recognises, if **“Buttons sound”** is set to **“Yes”** in the Settings menu. A gesture that the screen in front of you does not use gives no click. Alarms have their own sound, and the panel beeps when seeding starts and stops.

## Built into the panel

| **Part** | **What you see of it** |
| --- | --- |
| **microSD card slot** | A card symbol on the home screen while a card is ready. Reports are written to the card. See [Memory card](indicators.md#memory-card). |
| **Clock with its own battery** | The time of day at the top right of the home screen. A weak clock battery shows as **BAT**. See [Clock](indicators.md#clock). |
| **GPS receiver** | A satellite symbol in the right-hand column of the home screen, with the number of satellites. It can serve as the speed source. See [GPS](gps.md). |

> **TIP**
>
> **A touch the panel does not expect is ignored, not misread.** Each screen listens only to its own gestures. If nothing happens, check the gesture — a swipe too short, a finger lifted too early — before you suspect the panel.
