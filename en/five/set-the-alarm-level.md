# Set the alarm level automatically

Let the panel set the alarm level of the diagrams from the rate the machine really sows, with one double tap — grain drills in the "Flow" or "Hybrid" mode.

On a grain drill the seeds arrive too close together to be counted one by one, so the panel guards each row against an **alarm level**: the line on the diagram. Set it too high and every row sinks under the line — **“NOT UNDER CONTROL”**. Set it too low and a half-blocked row goes unnoticed. The panel can set it for you from what the machine really sows.

This works in the **“Flow”** and **“Hybrid”** modes, for the seed and the dry fertilizer diagrams, and for liquid fertilizer in the “Flow” mode. In the “Precise” mode the double tap changes nothing.

::: steps
1. Start seeding at your normal speed, with product flowing through every row.

2. **Double tap** the home screen with one finger — two quick taps on the same place.

3. The panel beeps once and lights the rate field: the new alarm level is set from the rate being sown now — **half** of it in the “Flow” mode, **one tenth** of it in the “Hybrid” mode.

   ![The diagrams after the double tap: the columns above the line, the rate field lit.](figures/five-home-auto-level)

4. Check that every column stands above its line. The light goes out by itself after a few seconds.
:::

The double tap works only while product is being applied. Standing still, nothing happens.

> **TIP**
>
> **Repeat it after a change of seed lot or speed.** The level follows the rate at the moment of the tap. You can also correct it by hand: touch and hold the diagram, then swipe up or down — see [Change the target rate](set-the-target-rate.md).

What the line means and why rows under it are not watched: [The section diagram](section-diagram.md#the-line-rows-under-it-are-not-under-control).
