# The machine page

The devices of the machine, the seed rate with − and +, the tramline and the way to the device settings, on one page.

**“Machine”** shows everything on the seeder that is not a row: the devices on the HUB-8 hub, the rate, the tramline. On an iPad held sideways it has four blocks; on an iPhone and on an iPad held upright the blocks stand one under another.

::: {.anatomy fig="app-machine"}
1. **The page title.**
2. **“Devices”** — every device of the machine as a tile: its name, its state or value, and an icon in a ring coloured by its state. A fan and a metering unit turn while they work. Tap a tile to open the page of the device.
3. **“Motors · rate”** — the set rate of the seed group in Hybrid or Precision, large, with what it is under it. Tap the number to open the rate sheet.
4. **− / +** — change the rate by one step; hold for ten steps. Each step goes to the gateway at once.
5. **“Sections and tramline”** — the tramline bout, the rhythm and the actuators, when the machine has a tramline; a dash without one. [Tramline](tramline.md)
6. **“Device settings”** › **“Open in Settings”** — opens **“Settings”** › **“Machine”**.
7. **A device in alarm** — here the hopper is **“Empty”**: the ring is red and blinks.

The machine page of a bench machine with a fan, a metering unit, a hopper, the work position and a flowmeter.
:::

## When there is nothing to show

| The page says | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **“The bridge is not connected”** | The app has no link to the gateway. | See [First connection to the gateway](first-connection.md). |
| **“No devices in the profile”** | The machine profile has no HUB-8 devices. | Add them: [HUB-8 inputs](hub8-inputs.md). |

The device states and their colours are the same as on the tiles of the rows screen. [The tiles at the bottom](bottom-tiles.md)
