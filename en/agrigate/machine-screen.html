The app

The machine page

ExplainedPre-release

The devices of the machine, the seed rate with − and +, the tramline and the way to the device settings, on one page.

“Machine” shows everything on the seeder that is not a row: the devices on the HUB-8 hub, the rate, the tramline. On an iPad held sideways it has four blocks; on an iPhone and on an iPad held upright the blocks stand one under another.

When there is nothing to show

The page says Why What to do
“The bridge is not connected” The app has no link to the gateway. See First connection to the gateway.
“No devices in the profile” The machine profile has no HUB-8 devices. Add them: HUB-8 inputs.
