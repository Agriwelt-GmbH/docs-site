# The help inside the app

Where the short help topics inside the Hectare app are, what they cover, and where the full help is.

The app carries five short help topics that work without a network. Open **“Settings”** › **“App”** › **“Help”**, then tap a topic; **‹** goes back to the list.

| Topic in the app | What it explains | More in this help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **“Bar colours and the three reds”** | What the colours of a row's bar mean, and the three different reds. | [Bar colours](bar-colours.md) |
| **“The line, by finger”** | How to move the control line by pressing and dragging the rate column. | [The rows screen](rows-screen.md) |
| **“Disable control”** | How to open the row menu and take a row off control, and how to put it back. | [The row menu](row-menu.md) |
| **“Alarm window”** | What the alarm window shows, how rows join it, and where the volume is set. | [The alarm window](alarm-window.md) |
| **“Bridge”** | What the gateway is, where its state is shown, and how to pick your gateway from the list. | [First connection to the gateway](first-connection.md) |

The help is reached only from the settings; the alarm window has no link to it. The service codes are not in the app's help; they are here: [How service codes work](codes.md).
