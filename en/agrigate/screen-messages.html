The app

Messages over the screen

TroubleshootingPre-release

The red and yellow capsules that appear over every page — the link to the gateway, the sound, and seeding without control — what each means and what to do.

Besides the alarm window, the app speaks through small capsules over the page. They are not windows: they need no closing and disappear when the cause is gone.

Red capsule in the middle of the screen — the link to the gateway

While the red capsule is shown, the app has no live data: the page is dimmed and shows the last reading.

The capsule says What it means What to do
“Looking for the bridge…” The app is looking for its gateway. Check that the gateway has power and the tablet is close to it.
“Connecting to” and a name The gateway is found; the app is connecting. Wait.
“Connection to” a name “lost, searching…” The link dropped; the app is looking for the same gateway again. Wait; check the gateway's power. After 3 seconds the window “Bridge link lost” opens. Code 601
“Bridge is silent…” The link is there, but the gateway sends nothing. Wait 10 seconds; if it stays, switch the gateway's power off and on. Code 602
“Choose bridge” Several gateways are in range, or yours is not found. Tap the capsule to open the list. Tap your gateway. First connection
“Bluetooth is off” Bluetooth is off on the tablet. Switch it on. Code 704
“No Bluetooth permission” The app may not use Bluetooth. Allow it in the iPad settings. Code 705

Yellow capsule at the top — the sound

The capsule says What it means What to do
“Volume is off” The tablet's volume is below 20 %: an alarm would not be heard. Turn the volume up. Code 701
“Sound in headphones” The sound goes to headphones, a Bluetooth speaker or another device. Disconnect it. Code 702

The same yellow line appears inside the alarm window, so that you know why you did not hear it.

Yellow capsule over a band — control

The capsule says What it means What to do
“NOT UNDER CONTROL” and the rows of the band, blinking Seed flows in the band, but fewer of its rows sow within the norm than the set share. The buzzer sounds. Check the control line or the rate and the yellow and dark-red rows. Code 207
