About the pictures

ReferencePre-release

The pictures in this help were taken from the app while it is being built, on Agriwelt's test bench — what in them is not from a real field.

The pictures in this help are screenshots of the Hectare app in English, taken on an iPad while the app is being built. The app is pre-release: a screen can look slightly different in the version you have.

Most pictures were taken on Agriwelt's test bench, not in a field. You can see that in a few places:

What you see in a picture Why
The yellow capsule “Volume is off” at the top of almost every screen The bench iPad runs with its volume down. In the cab this capsule is a warning that you would not hear an alarm — see Messages over the screen.
The word “bench” under the speed The speed is simulated on the bench. In the cab the speed comes from the iPad's GNSS.
Rows with faults placed on purpose — a silent row, a blocked row, rows in the tramline The bench shows every state of a row at once.
Very large areas, distances and seed counts on the statistics screen The bench has been running for many hours.
A machine with 7 rows and a working width of 25 m A bench setting, not a real seeder.

A few pictures were taken earlier in the development and have been cut to the part that is still current; the explanation under each picture says what to look at.
