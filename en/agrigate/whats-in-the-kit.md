# What is in the kit

The parts of an Agrigate system — the gateway, the Hectare app and the Agriwelt devices on the seeder — and what this pre-release version does not list yet.

An Agrigate system is made of the parts below. The gateway and the app are new; everything on the seeder is the same Agriwelt equipment that works with the Hectare monitors.

| Part | Where it sits | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The gateway** (**“Bridge”** in the app) | In the cab | Reads every device on the sensor line and talks to the app by Bluetooth. No screen, no buttons to use in work. |
| **The Hectare app** | On an iPad or iPhone in the cab | Shows and judges the rows, raises the alarms, keeps the statistics, sets up the gateway and the sensors. |
| **Seed sensors** | One in the seed tube of every row | Count the seeds of their row. The same sensor type watches fertilizer rows. |
| **HUB-8 hub** (only when needed) | On the seeder | Brings the other devices of the machine onto the sensor line: hopper level, fan speed, metering unit rotation, work position, tractor inputs, flowmeter. |
| **Tramline actuators** (only on machines with tramlining) | On the seeder | Shut the rows of the tramline. |

The seed sensors are connected one after another in a chain. A HUB-8 is needed only for the extra devices of the machine; a seeder with seed sensors alone has none. See [HUB-8 inputs](hub8-inputs.md).

## What this version does not list yet

> **NOTE**
>
> **The cables and the power connection of the kit are not listed in this pre-release version.** Neither are the dimensions and the protection rating of the gateway. Do not wire a gateway from a drawing of another product — ask Agriwelt for the cable set of your machine.

The app is not in the App Store. During the pre-release Agriwelt provides it.
