# Settings

The five groups of the settings — Products, Control and Alarms, Machine, App, Service — and what is in each.

Open the settings with the gear at the right of the top bar, or with **“Settings”** on the tab bar of an iPhone. On an iPad the groups stand in a list at the left and the open group at the right; on an iPhone a group opens over the list. A change takes effect at once — there is no Save button.

::: {.anatomy fig="app-settings"}
1. **“Products”** — what we sow: the control mode, the fraction, the rate. [Products](settings-products.md)
2. **“Control and Alarms”** — how the rows are judged: the share of seeding rows, the alarm delay, the tolerance, the buzzer. [Control and Alarms](settings-control-alarms.md)
3. **“Machine”** — the machine profile, the devices, the tramline and the gateway. It is open here. [Machine](settings-machine.md)
4. **“App”** — language, sound of the buttons, screen, home page, help and version. [App](settings-app.md)
5. **“Service”** — locked, for the Agriwelt service.
6. **“Machine profile”** — the rows and the fertilizer sensors. [Set up the machine profile](machine-profile.md)
7. **The profile status line** — what the gateway answered, here **“profile read from the bridge at”** and the time.
8. **“Number the sensors”** — [Number the sensors](sensor-numbering.md)
9. **HUB-8** — the devices of the machine. [HUB-8 inputs](hub8-inputs.md)

The settings of the group **“Machine”** on an iPad.
:::

The settings that the gateway must know — the profile, the fraction, the rate — are sent to it by the app. While the link to the gateway is down, the control mode, the fraction and the rate are greyed out, and the sensors cannot be numbered.
