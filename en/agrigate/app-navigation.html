The app

Moving around the app — the top bar and the page ring

ExplainedPre-release

The five pages of the Hectare app on a ring, the top bar of the iPad, the tab bar of the iPhone, and what Home opens.

The app has five pages — “Rows”, “Table”, “Statistics”, “Field” and “Machine” — and two places that are not pages: the list of “Alarms” and the “Settings”.

The top bar on the iPad

The page ring

The pages stand on a ring. Swipe left to go to the next page, right to go back: “Rows” → “Table” → “Statistics” → “Field” → “Machine” → “Rows” again. A swipe must be a clear sideways flick; pressing and dragging on a page (the control line, a row) does not turn the page.

The tab bar on the iPhone

On an iPhone — and on an iPad when the app has only a narrow part of the screen — the top bar is replaced by a tab bar at the bottom of the screen with four tabs:

Tab What it holds
“Seeding” The ring “Rows” → “Table” → “Statistics” → “Field”, turned by a swipe; dots under the page show where you are. Tap the lit tab again to go to the home page.
“Machine” The page “Machine”.
“Alarms” The list of active alarms, with the number of alarms on the tab.
“Settings” The settings.

What lies over every page

Whatever page is open, three things can appear over it: the alarm window (The alarm window), a red capsule about the link to the gateway and a yellow capsule about the sound (Messages over the screen).

The list of alarms

“Alarms” shows one line per kind of alarm, with the rows or devices it concerns — the same words as the alarm window. With nothing active it says “No active alarms”. Past alarms are not kept.
