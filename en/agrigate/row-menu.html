The app

The row menu

ExplainedPre-release

Everything about one row — its state in words, its readings, taking it off control, replacing its sensor and switching its product application off.

The row menu opens from the rows screen — press and hold a bar, slide to the row and lift the finger, or tap a band and then a row — and from the table, by tapping a line. Its title is “Row” with the number, or “Fertilizer · row” for a fertilizer row. “Done” closes it.

What you see

Part What it shows
The first line The state of the row in words and colour — for example “Normal”, “Below the line”, “Not seeding”, “Silent”, “Clogged” — and its rate.
“Precision seeding” (Precision and Hybrid only) “Seeds per metre”, “Thousand seeds/ha”, “Deviation from rate”, and in Precision “Quality, %”, “Doubles, %”, “Skips, %”. “All rows as a table” opens the table.
“Sensor settings” “Fraction” — the same value for every row of the group (“For every seed row.”). See Products settings.
“Information” “Rate” in seeds per second, “Control line”, “Seeds counted”, “Time between seeds”, “Sensor” — what the sensor reports — and “Last answered”.

What you can do

To do this Do this What happens
Stop watching a row whose fault you know “Disable control” The row stays on screen white with a “!”, raises no alarms and does not count toward the share of seeding rows.
Watch it again “Enable control” The row counts again toward control and alarms.
Put a new sensor on this row “Replace the sensor” The numbering sheet opens with this row's number already set. See Number the sensors.
Switch the product off on this row “Product application” off The row is treated as a tramline row: no “Not seeding”, out of the share of seeding rows, and “Seeding in tramline” if it still sows.
IMPORTANT

A row taken off control is blind, not quiet. Nothing about it is watched — not even a lost sensor — until you put it back with “Enable control”.

“Replace the sensor” is not available while the link to the gateway is down.
