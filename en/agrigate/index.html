Service codes

  • How service codes workWhat the three digits of an Agrigate service code mean, how the place is shown beside it, the four classes and how each looks in the app, and how to find a code from the words on the screen.
  • 1xx · Row sensor faultsCodes 101–106: a seed or fertilizer sensor of a row is silent, blocked, dazzled, faulty or is not a seed sensor.
  • 2xx · Seeding alarmsCodes 201–207: the sensor works, but the row sows too little, too much, with doubles or skips, into the tramline, or without control.
  • 3xx · Hopper, fan, metering unit and tractor inputsCodes 301–311: the devices on the HUB-8 hub — hopper, fan, metering unit, work position and tractor inputs.
  • 4xx · Tramline actuatorsCodes 401–404: a tramline actuator is silent, is the wrong device, does not know its position or is jammed.
  • 6xx · Gateway and linkCodes 601–602: the Bluetooth link to the gateway is lost, or the gateway is connected but sends nothing.
  • 7xx · iPad and iPhoneCodes 701–705: the tablet itself — volume, headphones, GNSS speed and Bluetooth.
  • 8xx · Gateway updateCodes 801–810: the steps and the results of updating the gateway's firmware from the app.
  • 9xx · Setup answers of the gatewayCodes 901–921: the gateway refused or could not finish writing the machine profile, a setting or a sensor number.
