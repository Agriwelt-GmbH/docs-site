# What you need — iPad or iPhone

Which iPad or iPhone runs the Hectare app, why the Hybrid and Precision modes need an iPad with GNSS, and how to set the tablet up so that no alarm goes unheard.

## The tablet

| What | Requirement | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device | iPad or iPhone | The app runs on both. On an iPad the pages sit under a top bar; on an iPhone under the tab bar at the bottom. See [Moving around the app](app-navigation.md). |
| System | iPadOS or iOS 18 or later | The app is built for this version and later. |
| GNSS | An iPad with GNSS (the Cellular models) for **“Hybrid”** and **“Precision”** | Seeds per metre need the ground speed, and the app takes it from the tablet's GNSS. Without it only the Flow mode (seeds per second) is offered. |
| Bluetooth | On, and allowed for the Hectare app | The app reaches the gateway only by Bluetooth. |
| Location | Allowed for the Hectare app (for Hybrid and Precision) | Without it the tablet gives no speed. |

When an iPad gives no GNSS speed, **“Settings”** › **“Products”** says so: **“This iPad gave no GNSS speed — Hybrid and Precision are unavailable. They need an iPad with GNSS (Cellular) and Location allowed.”**

## Set the tablet up so that you hear every alarm

The alarm buzzer plays through the tablet's speaker.

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **The tablet's volume decides whether you hear an alarm.** The side switch does not silence it, but a volume at zero does. Below 20 % the app shows **“Volume is off”**; with headphones or a Bluetooth speaker connected it shows **“Sound in headphones”**.

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Volume | Up. Check it with **“Test sound”** in **“Settings”** › **“Control and Alarms”**. |
| Headphones, external speakers | Not connected. |
| Screen | Kept on: switch on **“Keep screen awake”** in **“Settings”** › **“App”**, or set the tablet's own Auto-Lock to Never. |
| Bluetooth | On. |
| The tablet in the cab | Close to the gateway. Every message about the link asks you to keep the tablet near the gateway. |

The app does not send notifications; Do Not Disturb and Focus do not change the alarm.
