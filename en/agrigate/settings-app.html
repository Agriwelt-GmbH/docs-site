The app

Settings — App

ReferencePre-release

The language of the app, the button click, keeping the screen awake, what Home opens, the built-in help and the version.

“Settings” › “App” holds the settings of the app itself. They are kept on the tablet, not in the gateway.

Section Setting What it does
“Language and appearance” “Language” “System” (the tablet's language), Ukrainian, German or English. The app changes language at once.
“Theme” “Dark · for the cab” — the only theme.
“Sound” “Buttons sound” A click on every button. Off unless switched on.
“Work screen” “Keep screen awake” The tablet does not dim or lock its screen while the app is open.
— “Home screen of the Seeding tab” What “Home” and the start of the app open: “Rows”, or “Last opened” — the page of the ring you were on last, also after a restart.
— “Help” The built-in help. The built-in help
“About” “Version” The version of the app. Give it to the service with the gateway's firmware version.
TIP

Keep the screen on in the cab — with “Keep screen awake”, or with the tablet's own Auto-Lock set to Never. A screen that goes dark hides the alarm window.
