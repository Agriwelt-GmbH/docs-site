# The field picture

The seeder drawn in perspective with every row painting the ground behind it in the colour of its bar — the last metres of every row at a glance.

**“Field”** draws the seeder as you would see it from the cab, standing on a field that moves under it. Every seed row is a column on the bar of the seeder — the same bar as on **“Rows”**, with its height and colour. Behind every row the ground is painted in the colour the row shows, and the painted strip runs away to the horizon at the speed of the machine. So you see the last metres of every row at a glance: a row that stopped leaves a gap that moves away from the seeder.

![The page “Field” from behind, with the tiles under it.](figures/app-field)

The picture is drawn from what the rows and the speed show at this moment. It is not a map: nothing is stored, and it has no GNSS track.

## What you can do

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Look from another side | Tap the picture. It turns through three views: from above, from behind, and from the front looking back (**“Looking back”** is written on it). The iPad keeps the last view. |
| See the fertilizer rows | With fertilizer sensors in the profile, switch between **“Seed”** and **“Fertilizer”** at the top. A red dot on the other choice tells you that a row of that group is red. |
| Use the tiles | The strip under the picture is the same as under **“Rows”**. [The tiles at the bottom](bottom-tiles.md) |

> **NOTE**
>
> **Without a speed the field still moves.** A Flow machine needs no speed, so the picture then moves at a fixed pace while seed flows. The distance in the picture is real only when there is a speed.

The alarms are not on this page: the alarm window opens over it as over every page. [The alarm window](alarm-window.md)
