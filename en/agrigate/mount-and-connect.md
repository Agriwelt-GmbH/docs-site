# Mount and connect the gateway

Where the gateway goes, what it is connected to, and what this pre-release version does not describe yet about cables and power.

## Where the gateway goes

The gateway goes **in the cab**, close to where the iPad or iPhone is mounted. The tablet reaches the gateway by Bluetooth: the shorter the distance, the steadier the link. Every message of the app about a lost or broken link asks you to keep the tablet near the gateway.

## What it is connected to

The gateway takes the place of the Hectare monitor at the cab end of the sensor line. It is connected to two things:

- **the sensor line of the seeder** — the same line of seed sensors, HUB-8 and tramline actuators that a Hectare monitor would be connected to;
- **the power of the tractor.**

The sensor line on the seeder itself — the sensors one after another in a chain, the HUB-8 where one is used — is laid out as for a Hectare monitor.

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **This pre-release version does not describe the cables, the connectors and the power connection of the gateway.** They are not final. Have the gateway connected by Agriwelt or by a fitter who has the cable set and the instructions for your machine. Do not connect the gateway to a socket or a cable meant for another device.

## Check after connecting

The connection is right when the app, with the gateway powered:

1. finds the gateway and connects to it — see [First connection to the gateway](first-connection.md);
2. shows **“Sensors on the bus now”** in **“Settings”** › **“Machine”** with the number of devices you expect on the seeder — see [Set up the machine profile](machine-profile.md);
3. shows every row of the machine on the rows screen, none of them with a red ✕ — see [Bar colours](bar-colours.md).
