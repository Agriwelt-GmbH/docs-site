# Set the date and time

Set the clock of the Hectare Mini by hand, or let it follow the GPS receiver with a time zone.

The panel stamps the start and end of the work on every field with its clock, and shows the time on the home screen. **“Change Date&Time”** is in the **“Settings”** menu at the **“Operator”** level. What it opens depends on the **“GPS”** item of the [profile](profile.md).

## With GPS in the profile

::: {.anatomy fig="menu-datetime"}
1. **“Automatic”** — whether the clock follows the GPS receiver.
2. **Its value**, here **“No”**. Change it with **“PLUS”** / **“MINUS”**.
3. **“Enter date and time”** — opens the menu for setting the clock by hand. Shown while **“Automatic”** is **“No”**.

The date and time menu of a panel with the built-in GPS receiver switched on.
:::

- **“Automatic”** at **“Yes”**: the clock takes the time from the GPS receiver. The item **“Time zone”** appears instead of **“Enter date and time”** — set your offset from UTC with **“PLUS”** / **“MINUS”**, from −12:00 to +14:00 in whole hours.
- **“Automatic”** at **“No”**: select **“Enter date and time”** and press **“MENU”**, then go on as below from step 2.

## By hand

::: steps
1. **“MENU”** → **“Settings”** → **“Change Date&Time”**, press **“MENU”**. Without GPS in the profile the menu opens with **“Year”**, **“Month”**, **“Day”**, **“Hours”**, **“Minutes”**, **“Seconds”** and **“Set”**.

2. Select an item with **“UP”** / **“DOWN”** and change it with **“PLUS”** / **“MINUS”**. **“MENU”** on a number — all items but **“Month”** — opens the [number pad](number-pad.md).

3. Set all six items. After a change of the month, a day that the month does not have is pulled back to its last day.

4. Select **“Set”** and press **“MENU”**. The clock takes the new date and time, and the menu you came from returns.
:::

**“BACK”** leaves without changing the clock. Every item opens with the present date and time, so you only change what is wrong.
