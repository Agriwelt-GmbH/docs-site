How to…

  • Change the target rate or the alarm levelRaise or lower the alarm level, the target rate in seeds per metre or the product rate in kg/ha — on the home screen or on the rate control screen.
  • Set the alarm level of a grain drillLet the Hectare Mini set the alarm level from the flow being applied, with one long press — or set it by hand — so that a line that loses flow is reported.
  • Lock product application by handStop the dispenser motors and the liquid pump with one long press, so that nothing turns while you work on the machine — and how the panel shows the lock.
  • Take a sensor into or out of controlSwitch the watching of a single sensor, motor or actuator off and on with the sensor setup mode — what the panel asks, and when it wants the statistics erased.
  • Zero the readings of the Seeding tableSet the counters and interval figures of the Seeding table to zero — what is zeroed, what is not, and what else zeroes them.
  • Start work on a new fieldChoose the field the panel writes its statistics to, and erase it if it is not empty — before the first pass on a new field.
  • Fill the disks with seedsTurn the seed disks of a planter with dispenser motors until every disk holds seed, so that sowing starts from the first metre — how to run it and read the result.
  • Assign a product to a dispenser motorTell the panel which of its three products each dispenser motor applies, so that one target rate in kg/ha serves all motors of that product.
  • Calibrate a dispenser motor by weightTell the panel how much product a motor-driven dispenser delivers per shaft turn — what you need, the steps on the screen, and what the failure messages mean.
  • Set up the tramline rhythmTell the Hectare Mini on which bouts which actuator or motor shuts off — the limits, the rhythm editor, its dialogs, and the order that keeps your rhythm from being overwritten.
  • Tramline in the field — bout indicator and next boutRead the tramline indicator, move to the next bout by hand or let the panel do it from the markers or the implement position, and recognise a line shut off for the tramline.
