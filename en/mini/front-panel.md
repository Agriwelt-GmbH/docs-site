# The panel and its seven buttons

What the Hectare Mini panel consists of, how it is mounted, and what each of the seven buttons does with a short and a long press.

The Hectare Mini panel has a 320 × 240 colour screen and seven buttons on the frame around it. The buttons are touch areas, not mechanical keys: a short touch is a short press; a touch held for about half a second is a long press.

::: {.anatomy fig="panel-callouts"}
1. **Power button.** Touch-sensitive, at the top edge of the housing.
2. **Bracket.** Lets you mount the panel on a flat surface at any angle, horizontally or vertically. For mounting on a tube, an additional bracket is available.
3. **The seven buttons.** Three on the left, three on the right, one under the screen — see the table below.
4. **Screen.** It also takes touches in some places, for example the digits of the [number pad](number-pad.md).
5. **Cable entry.** One harness brings the speed sensor, the sensor network and the power supply to the panel.

The panel from the front and from the side, and its harness.
:::

## The seven buttons

| **Button** | **Short press** | **Long press** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ![](figures/icons/key-home.png){height=34px} **“HOME”** | Moves on to the next working screen. In an alarm window: closes the alarm. | On the home screen and the rate control screen: locks or unlocks product application, if the machine has dispenser motors or a liquid fertilizer pump. In an alarm window: closes it and returns to the home screen. |
| ![](figures/icons/key-back.png){height=34px} **“BACK”** | Leaves a menu, a window or a mode, one step back. On the home screen: opens the tramline bout menu, if the tramline is on. | On the home screen, while seeding: sets the alarm level from the flow being applied — see [Set the alarm level](set-the-alarm-level.md). |
| ![](figures/icons/key-minus.png){height=34px} **“MINUS”** | Lowers the selected value. In the sensor setup mode: takes the selected sensor out of control. | — |
| ![](figures/icons/key-menu.png){height=34px} **“MENU”** | Opens the menu of the screen, or enters the selected menu item. In the sensor setup mode: opens the context menu of the selected device. | On the home screen, the rate control screen and the Motors screen: switches on the sensor setup mode. |
| ![](figures/icons/key-plus.png){height=34px} **“PLUS”** | Raises the selected value. In the sensor setup mode: takes the selected sensor into control. | — |
| ![](figures/icons/key-down.png){height=34px} **“DOWN”** | Selects the next item, field or device. | — |
| ![](figures/icons/key-up.png){height=34px} **“UP”** | Selects the previous item, field or device. | — |

> **NOTE**
>
> **Holding a button does not repeat it.** One touch is one step, however long you hold it — a held button either does its long-press function or nothing. To change a value by ten steps, touch ten times. Where a value is large, the [number pad](number-pad.md) is quicker.

If **“Buttons sound”** is set to **“Yes”** in the [Settings menu](settings-menu.md), the panel beeps at every touch it accepts.
