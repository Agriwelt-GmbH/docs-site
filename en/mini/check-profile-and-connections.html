Setting up

Check the profile and the connections

How toHectare Mini firmware · 2.2.8

The first check after installing a Hectare Mini — does the profile match the machine, does every device answer, is every sensor under control — as a procedure with what the screen shows at each step.

The panel watches only the devices its profile lists. Systems are usually delivered with the profile already set, but before you set any parameter, check that it matches the machine and that every device answers. You need no access code for this check.

1. Check the profile

  1. Connect all devices to the panel and power them as the wiring diagram in the data sheet of the system shows.

  2. Hold “UP” and switch the panel on. The splash screen appears, then the profile view. Release the button.

  3. Read the profile view, from the top:

    • the name of the panel with the software version, in green, and SN# with the serial number;
    • the line “Speed” with the way the speed sensor is connected: “Control panel”, “Input device” or “Input device (2)”;
    • every kind of device in the profile with its number, or “Yes” for a device that exists only once, such as the work position sensor.

    “UP” and “DOWN” scroll the list when it is longer than the screen.

  4. Compare the list with the machine and the wiring diagram.

  5. Wait until the hint “BACK - exit” appears at the bottom — the panel shows it once the devices have answered, or after 15 seconds at the latest. Press “BACK”. The home screen opens.

If the profile does not match the machine, set it up first: Set up the profile.

2. Check that every device answers

Wait a few seconds on the home screen. If no window “Connection lost” appears, the panel has found every device of the profile.

When several devices of one kind do not answer, one window lists all their numbers — here pressure sensors 1 to 3:

IMPORTANT

A device that does not answer is not watched. Check its connection against the wiring diagram and the profile once more. If you cannot find the cause, call the service — see Device does not answer.

3. Check that every sensor is under control

A sensor out of control is not watched at all. Its indicator shows the blinking grey not-controlled sign, or !!!! in place of the value; under a diagram column it shows a white box with ! — see Indicators.

If there is such a sensor, take it into control:

  1. On the home screen hold “MENU” until the speed indicator has a white background. The panel is in the sensor setup mode. Release the button.

  2. Press “DOWN” (or “UP”) until the sensor out of control is selected. A seed sensor is selected when its number under the column has a white background.

  3. Press “PLUS”. The panel asks whether you are sure; press “MENU” for “Yes”. “BACK” answers “No”.

  4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 for every sensor out of control. The question comes only the first time while you stay in this mode.

  5. Press “BACK” to leave the sensor setup mode.

Switching the control of a sensor on is what this manual calls taking a sensor into control. More: Take a sensor into or out of control.

The result

Every sensor must be under control, and no indicator may show a red cross — the sign of a device that does not answer.

When everything is right, the home screen shows every device in its normal grey, with no red cross and no not-controlled sign:

Then go on with the settings: Commissioning.
