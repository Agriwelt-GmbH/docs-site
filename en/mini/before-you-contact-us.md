# Before you contact support

What to check, what to write down and which photos to take before you ask for help with a Hectare Mini — and whom to ask first.

Most faults can be found with the manual. Go through this list first; with it, support can help you faster.

## Checklist

- The [check after installation](check-after-installation.md) was done, and you know which step failed.
- You looked up the text of the alarm in the alarm topics: [How alarms work](how-alarms-work.md), [Device does not answer](alarms-any-device.md), [Seeding alarms](alarms-seeding.md), [Seed sensor state](alarms-sensor-state.md), [Fan or dispenser shaft](alarms-rotation.md), [Hopper empty](alarms-hopper.md), [Pressure alarms](alarms-pressure.md), [Speed sensor alarms](alarms-speed-sensor.md).
- You did the checks that the topic gives, in the order it gives them.

## What to write down

- **The exact text of the alarm window**, with the kind of device and its number — as on the screen.
- **The firmware version** — [Check after installation, step 7](check-after-installation.md#record-the-result).
- **The machine and the number of rows.**
- **What was done before the fault** — a repair, a new sensor, a changed setting, a new field.

## Which photos to attach

- The **sensor status screen** of the sensor concerned — [The sensor status screen](sensor-status-screen.md).
- The **alarm window**.
- **How the devices are connected**: the connectors and the harness, so that every plug can be seen.

## Whom to contact

1. **The dealer or the partner who installed the system.** They know your machine and give the PIN of your panel.
2. **Agriwelt**, if the dealer cannot help: **support@agriwelt.com**. Send the notes and the photos from the lists above in one message.

The contact details of the service are also in the data sheet of the system.
