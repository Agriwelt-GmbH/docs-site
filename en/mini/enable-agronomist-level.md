# Enable the Agronomist level

The steps, with the screen of each, to raise the access level of the Hectare Mini to "Agronomist" or "Service" before changing settings, and to return to "Operator" afterwards.

Sensor parameters, the working width, the calibrations and the device configuration need the **“Agronomist”** level. The **“Service”** level is raised the same way. What each level opens: [Access levels](access-levels.md).

You need the PIN of this panel for that level. It is different for every panel; the dealer gives it.

::: steps
1. On the home screen press **“MENU”**. The main menu opens.

   ![The main menu.](figures/menu-main)

2. Select **“Settings”** with **“UP”** / **“DOWN”** and press **“MENU”**. The **“Settings”** menu opens.

3. Press **“DOWN”** until **“User”** is selected — it is the last item. Press **“PLUS”** until it reads **“Agronomist”** (or **“Service”**).

   ![“User” set to Agronomist.](figures/menu-settings-user)

4. Press **“MENU”**. The PIN pad opens with **“Enter your PIN:”**; the line under the prompt identifies this panel. Type the PIN on the [number pad](number-pad.md) and touch **“OK”** or press **“MENU”**.

   ![The PIN pad.](figures/user-agronom-pin)

5. The panel shows the name of the new level for a moment and returns to the **“Settings”** menu, now with the items of that level — for example **“Seeding machine parameters”**.

   ![The panel confirms the new level.](figures/user-agronom-done)

6. Press **“BACK”** to leave the menu.
:::

If the PIN is wrong — or is the PIN of another level than the one chosen in step 3 — the panel shows **“Invalid PIN”** for a moment and opens the PIN pad again. Type it again, or press **“BACK”** to give up; the level stays as it was.

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **At “Agronomist” and “Service” no alarm windows are shown.** When you have finished, go back to **“Operator”**: repeat steps 1–3, choose **“Operator”** with **“MINUS”** and press **“MENU”** — no PIN is asked going down. Switching the panel off and on does the same.

When you come back down, the alarms that are still true are shown one after another — see [Access levels](access-levels.md#what-happens-to-the-alarms-while-the-level-is-high).
