# Before you contact support

What to check, what to write down and which photos to take before you ask for help, and whom to ask first.

Most faults can be found with the manual. If you still need help, a complete message gets an answer faster.

## Check first

- The [check after installation](check-after-installation.md) was passed, or you know which step failed.
- You have looked up the text of the alarm in the alarm topics ([How alarms work](how-alarms-work.md)).
- The faulty device answers: **“Link:”** on its status screen ([The sensor status screen](sensor-status-screen.md)), and the cable, the connector and the power supply are in order ([Any device: connection lost or wrong device type](alarms-any-device.md)).

## Write down

- The **exact text** of the alarm window, and the kind and number of the device named in it ([How alarms work](how-alarms-work.md)).
- The firmware version — where to read it: [Check after installation](check-after-installation.md), step 7.
- The machine and the number of rows.
- What was done before the fault appeared.
- The result of the check after installation, if you have recorded it.

## Take photos

- The sensor status screen of the faulty device ([The sensor status screen](sensor-status-screen.md)).
- The alarm window.
- How the devices are connected.

## Whom to contact

1. **Your dealer or the partner who installed the system.** Start here.
2. **Agriwelt:** support@agriwelt.com. Attach the notes and the photos.
