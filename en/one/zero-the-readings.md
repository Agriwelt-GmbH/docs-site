# Zero the readings of the Table screen

Set the seed and fertilizer counters of the Table screen to zero — for a new field or a new seed lot — without touching the field statistics.

The Table screen keeps counting from the moment its readings were last zeroed. Zero them when you start a new field or a new seed lot, so that the counters and deviations describe only the new work.

::: steps
1. Open the Table screen: **“HOME”** on the home screen.

2. Press **“MENU”**, or tap the screen with two fingers. The panel asks **“Zero readings?”**.

   ![The question before zeroing: Yes in green, No in red.](figures/question-zero-readings)

3. Touch **“Yes”**, or press **“PLUS”**. The counters of every seed, dry fertilizer and liquid fertilizer sensor are set to zero.
:::

**“No”**, **“MINUS”** or **“BACK”** leaves everything as it was.

**Only the counters of the Table screen are zeroed.** The [field statistics](statistics-screen.md) are not touched; they are erased with **“Zero statistics”** in the menu of the Statistics screen.

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **Zeroing cannot be undone.** Do it when you start something new, not to tidy the screen.
