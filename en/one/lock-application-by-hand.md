# Lock product application by hand

Stop all product application with one long press, and start it again — what the lock does and how to see that it is on.

The manual lock stops **all product application**: the dispenser motors and the pump do not run, however the machine moves. Use it to cross a field already sown, to drive on the road with the implement lowered, or when you work [without the speed sensor](work-without-speed-sensor.md).

::: steps
1. On the home screen, **hold “HOME”** until the panel beeps once.

2. Check the right edge of the screen. A **yellow hand** blinks there: application is locked.

   ![](figures/icons/hand.png){height=48px}

3. To unlock, hold **“HOME”** again. One short beep; the hand disappears.
:::

On the second home screen the same works with **“HOME”**, or by touching and holding the hand.

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **The lock is announced by the screen only.** Look for the yellow hand before you start a pass. A locked machine drives a whole field and sows nothing.

## What the lock changes elsewhere

- The motor tiles on the [Motors screen](motors-screen.md) show a yellow **STOP** — serviceable, not applying.
- The pump rate indicator shows a yellow pump with **STOP** ([Liquid fertilizer pump](indicators.md#liquid-fertilizer-pump)).
- The product application control does not start when you move off — the lock must be off for the control to start ([Quick start](quick-start.md#moving-off)).
- The work done while locked is counted in the statistics as **Work without alarms control** ([Statistics](statistics-screen.md)).

The yellow hand is shown on machines with electric dispenser motors or a liquid fertilizer pump.
