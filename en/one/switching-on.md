# Switching on

What the panel does after power-on, which access level it starts at, and what a long splash screen means.

After the power is switched on, a splash screen with the logo appears. It stays until the panel has found every device connected to it — a few seconds — and then the [home screen](home-screen.md) opens.

> **NOTE**
>
> **If the splash screen stays much longer than usual,** the panel is waiting for a device that does not answer. Nothing is broken yet — but look at the alarms as soon as the home screen appears. See [How alarms work](how-alarms-work.md).

## What state the panel starts in

- **Access level: Operator.** The panel starts at the Operator level after every power-on, whatever level was set before. Switching off and on is therefore also the quickest way back to Operator — the only level at which alarm windows are shown. See [Access levels](access-levels.md).
- **The ordinary home screen.** Even on a machine without seed sensors the panel starts on the ordinary home screen; the [second home screen](second-home-screen.md) is opened by hand.

## Switching off

The statistics are written every five minutes and when the machine stops. **Switch the panel off only after you have stopped moving** — otherwise the last stretch of work is lost from the [statistics](statistics-screen.md).
