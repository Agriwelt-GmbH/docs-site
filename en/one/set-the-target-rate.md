# Change the target rate

Raise or lower the rate the panel holds and guards, from the home screen, while seeding — with the buttons or by touch.

The rate field at the right of each diagram shows two numbers: **the measured rate on top, the target below it**. In the “Flow” mode the target is the alarm level of the diagram — see [The section diagram](section-diagram.md).

## With the buttons

::: steps
1. On the home screen, press **“PLUS”** or **“MINUS”** once. A rate field is selected and starts to blink; nothing has changed yet.

   ![The rate field at the right selected for editing: lit background, target in white.](figures/one-home-main-norm)

2. Press **“PLUS”** to raise the target or **“MINUS”** to lower it, one step per press.

3. If the machine has more than one rate field — seed and fertilizer, or products by weight — press **“DOWN”** or **“UP”** to move to the next or the previous one.

4. Press **“BACK”** to finish. The field stops blinking and the new target is in force.
:::

## By touch

::: steps
1. Touch and hold the rate field of a diagram. It lights up.

2. Swipe up over the screen to raise the target, down to lower it — one step per swipe.

3. Touch the screen outside the diagrams to finish.
:::

For a **product applied by weight**, touching and holding its kg/ha indicator opens the number pad instead: type the target and touch **“OK”** ([Entering numbers](interface-basics.md#entering-numbers)). On the [second home screen](second-home-screen.md), touch and hold the rate, then swipe.

> **IMPORTANT**
>
> **Columns under the line are not guarded.** If the target is set far above what the machine really delivers, the columns sink under the control line and the panel shows **“NOT UNDER CONTROL”**. On a grain drill, let the panel find the level: [Set the alarm level automatically](set-the-alarm-level.md).
